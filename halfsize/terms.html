2. How it works with your videos

The app creates new, smaller copies of the videos you choose, on your device, and saves them to your photo library. Compressing reduces resolution, frame rate or bitrate depending on the quality you choose, so a copy is not identical to its original. Originals are only removed when you confirm it in the iOS dialog and remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days; after that, or if you empty that album, they cannot be recovered by us. Review the copies before emptying Recently Deleted. Slow motion, Cinematic, spatial and other special videos are left untouched.
